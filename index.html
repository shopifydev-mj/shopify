Common questions about my work.

What makes Mark Jason Nastor a Senior Shopify Developer? +

Mark Jason Nastor is a Philippines-based Senior Shopify Developer, Shopify Select Partner, and AI Application Developer with 10+ years of web development experience and 300+ Shopify stores supported worldwide, backed by a 5.0 client rating. He specializes in Shopify Plus, CRO, site speed optimization, custom theme and app development, and LLM integrations, and he created ShoptimizePro, a publicly launched AI-powered Shopify app.

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines? +

Yes. I’m based in the Philippines, working remotely worldwide with ecommerce brands in the US, UK, Australia, and other markets. I support async workflows and timezone overlap depending on the project.

How long does a typical project take? +

A theme customization runs 1–2 weeks. A full custom build is usually 4–8 weeks depending on the number of templates and integrations. I share a timeline upfront and won't pad it.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +

Yes — most of my long-term clients are on a retainer for monthly updates, performance audits, A/B tests, and the occasional "something broke at 11pm."

Where are you based, and does timezone matter? +

I'm based in the Philippines (GMT+8), working remotely worldwide. I've worked with US, UK, and AU clients for years — I overlap mornings or evenings depending on the project, and async works great for everything else.

What Shopify services do you offer? +

I offer custom Shopify and Shopify Plus development (including Checkout Extensibility and Shopify Functions), Online Store 2.0 sections, redesigns and migrations, CRO and product-page optimization, site speed and Core Web Vitals work, Shopify app and API integrations, AI and LLM integrations, and ongoing monthly support.

Are you a Shopify developer or a Shopify agency? +

I’m an independent Senior Shopify Developer and Shopify Select Partner. Clients work directly with me, which keeps communication clear, technical execution fast, and project ownership simple.

How do we start? +

Send me a short message about what you're working on. I'll reply within 24 hours, and if we're a fit, we hop on a 30-min call to scope it out.
