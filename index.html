Frequently Asked

Common questions about my work.

What kind of Shopify work do you do? +

Full store builds, theme customization, Liquid development on OS 2.0, speed optimization, CRO improvements, app setup, and headless builds on Hydrogen when it's the right call. If it's on Shopify, I've probably done it.

How do you charge — hourly or fixed? +

Both, depending on scope. Ongoing maintenance and small tasks are hourly. Full builds and redesigns are fixed-price after a short scoping call, so you know exactly what you're paying for.

How long does a typical project take? +

A theme customization runs 1–2 weeks. A full custom build is usually 4–8 weeks depending on the number of templates and integrations. I share a timeline upfront and won't pad it.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +

Yes — most of my long-term clients are on a retainer for monthly updates, performance audits, A/B tests, and the occasional "something broke at 11pm."

Where are you based, and does timezone matter? +

I'm in Pangasinan, Philippines (GMT+8). I've worked with US, UK, and AU clients for years — I overlap mornings or evenings depending on the project, and async works great for everything else.

How do we start? +

Send me a short message about what you're working on. I'll reply within 24 hours, and if we're a fit, we hop on a 30-min call to scope it out.
